# Building Care Visual Unification Design

## Scope

Only `/building-care` changes. Existing copy, pricing, form behavior, and the other three landing pages remain unchanged.

## Visual direction

- Use `#F4F7FB` as the page canvas, white cards, `#0B2748` text, and `#1769FF` accents.
- Replace hard navy section blocks with photo cards, subtle navy overlays, light borders, and Toss-like soft shadows.
- Render the existing indoor stairwell team photo as a blended background in the shared intro, but only on the building-care page.
- Use campaign images featuring suited staff for service-scope cards.
- Use actual BRING CARE field records for facts and visit-result cards.

## Content mapping

- Facts: vacancy check, tenancy support, tenant notices, completed address-sign work.
- Visit results: common-area cleanup, defect check, completed work record.
- Scope: suited campaign visuals mapped to vacancy, common-area inspection, complaint connection, and minor repair.

## Responsive behavior

- Desktop cards retain their current grid counts.
- Mobile cards keep readable overlays and crop from the center without placing the intro image below the copy.

## Acceptance criteria

- Building-care shows all requested images and a unified light color system.
- Other landing pages retain their existing intro and section presentation.
- Existing tests, build, static export, and Firebase deployment succeed.
